Market data integrity

Token market cap and FDV are different metrics; when market cap is unavailable, FDV is labeled explicitly. Historical contract addresses may be different from the linked tradable token (wrapper / derivative). NFT floor prices and volumes use their reported payment currencies and cannot always be compared as ETH. OpenSea collection links from the original archive may refer to wrapped or related collections. No token/NFT should be presumed safe because it appears here. Missing provider data is shown as unavailable, never as zero.
